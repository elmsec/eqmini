# eqMini 0.4.0 — preview 6

The audio access explanation now appears only before you first enable EQ. Its acknowledgement is preserved when reopening or updating the app, including for users who accepted it in an earlier version. macOS continues to manage System Audio Recording permission separately.

Requires **Apple silicon** and **macOS 14.2+**. Quit eqMini before replacing it in Applications.

The app and DMG are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. ZIP and SHA-256 checksums are included.

Build 10. [Usage and source](https://github.com/elmsec/eqmini/blob/v0.4.0-preview.6/README.md).
