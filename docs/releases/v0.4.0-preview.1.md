# eqMini 0.4.0 — preview 1

A small macOS equalizer with system-wide or selected-app processing, Simple and 10-band controls, bass harmonics, and a menu bar panel plus regular window.

**Requirements:** Apple silicon Mac, macOS 14.2 or later.

**Signing status:** this is an ad-hoc signed preview, **not notarized**. A downloaded copy may be blocked by macOS.

## Install

1. Download the DMG and drag **eqMini** into **Applications**, or extract the ZIP and move the app there.
2. Open the app. If macOS blocks this preview and you choose to trust it, use the per-app **Open Anyway** control in Privacy & Security. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.
3. Enable the equalizer. eqMini explains the required **System Audio Recording** permission before continuing to macOS audio setup.

Audio stays on your Mac, is not saved, and is never uploaded. The app makes no network requests. Policy and support links open your browser.

Launch at login and Restore EQ on launch are both optional and off by default. Closing windows leaves eqMini in the menu bar; Quit stops processing.

## Included

- Flat, Bass Boost, Warm, saved Custom, and full reset.
- Simple Bass/Voices/Detail controls that follow the current EQ curve.
- Ten frequency bands, optional Speaker bass harmonics, Keep volume, and output gain.
- Selected-app routing, native window menus, Settings, About, and optional support links.
- ZIP, drag-to-Applications DMG, and SHA-256 checksum file.

See the [README](https://github.com/elmsec/eqmini/blob/v0.4.0-preview.1/README.md) for screenshots, privacy, source builds, and known limits.

## Validation and limits

Strong boosts may cause limiter compression; laptop speakers cannot reproduce physical sub-bass.

The stable bundle identifier is now `dev.elma.eqmini`. Prototype preferences migrate, but macOS may require a fresh audio permission grant.
