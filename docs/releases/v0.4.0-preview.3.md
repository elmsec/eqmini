# eqMini 0.4.0 — preview 3

- Right-click the menu-bar icon for Open Equalizer, Enable/Disable, Settings, About, and Quit. Left-click opens the panel.
- Opening eqMini shows its window; launch at login stays quiet.
- The first enable after an update shows the audio explanation, including when using the Equalizer menu. Not Now leaves EQ off.
- Pausing music keeps EQ enabled. The watchdog now checks source playback activity before treating missing callbacks as a stalled route.
- Audio Recording Settings appears only for permission-related failures.

Requires **Apple silicon** and **macOS 14.2+**. Drag eqMini from the DMG into Applications, replacing the previous copy after quitting it. ZIP and SHA-256 checksums are also included.

The app and DMG are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. System Audio Recording permission is still required for live EQ. Audio stays on your Mac and is not saved or uploaded.

Build 7. [Usage and source](https://github.com/elmsec/eqmini/blob/v0.4.0-preview.3/README.md).
